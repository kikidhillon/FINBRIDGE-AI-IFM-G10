## Numerically verified facts for narration

- MYR/USD **increases** = MYR depreciates. If MYR/USD decreases, MYR appreciates.
- Baseline: `PPP=4.544118`, `IRP=4.457143`, Malaysian real Fisher rate `0.970874%`.
- MY inflation increase to 6.5%: `PPP=4.698529` (MYR depreciates in conditional PPP), `IRP=4.457143` (unchanged).
- MY interest increase to 7%: `IRP=4.585714`, `PPP=4.544118` (unchanged).
- Covered IRP = *theoretical forward*; PPP/IFE = *model-dependent expected future spot*, NOT certainty. The IFE value happens to match IRP in the simplified algebra but is **not equivalent in risk or contract form**.
